Holder General Engineering a brand built to last

I designed the brand identity and marketing site for Holder General Engineering, a general engineering and heavy-civil contractor in Carlsbad, California, and built it in WordPress. The goal: a confident, industrial brand that signals reliability and scale, and a homepage that turns project owners into estimate requests.

01 · The brief

Look established

The work happens on job sites, but the first impression happens on a phone.

Holder General Engineering is a general engineering contractor working in site development and heavy civil: grading, underground utilities, concrete, and the groundwork larger projects are built on. As a newer company, they needed a brand and a website that could stand next to bigger, established firms and win the trust of project owners, developers, and general contractors.

The brief was simple to say and hard to earn: look established. Someone is often deciding who to call for an estimate from their phone, so the brand had to feel as solid as the work.

02 · Brand system

Legible on a hard hat or a handset

Industrial, confident, and legible at a glance. The system is built on a heavy, geometric wordmark, a deep navy, and a high-visibility amber pulled straight from the safety vocabulary of a job site. The tagline, "Built Right. Built to Last," does double duty as a promise and a positioning line.

03 · The website

One page, one job: earn the estimate request

The homepage leads with the work itself: real job-site imagery of grading and heavy equipment, with the tagline set large over the top. Trust signals sit where people look for them. A slim contact bar puts the phone number, email, and Carlsbad address in reach from the first scroll, and a standing "Get a Project Estimate" button follows down the page.

I built it in WordPress with Elementor and designed it mobile-first, because a lot of this audience is deciding who to call from a truck. The same hierarchy holds from desktop to phone: brand, promise, proof, and a clear next step.
