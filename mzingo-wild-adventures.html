Mzingo Wild Adventures: a brand for real Kenyan safaris

I designed the brand identity and marketing site for Mzingo Wild Adventures, a private safari company running tailor-made trips across Kenya. The site is live.

The goal was a brand that feels premium and trustworthy, warm enough to plan a once-in-a-lifetime trip with, and a site that turns browsers into quote requests.

The brief

Mzingo Wild Adventures runs private, tailor-made safaris across Kenya, built around each traveler and what the wildlife is actually doing on the ground. It sits in a crowded market full of look-alike tour operators and busy discount-booking sites.

The brief was to stand apart by feeling calm and premium. Someone is planning an expensive, far-away, once-in-a-lifetime trip, so the brand had to earn trust in seconds, and the site had to move that trust toward a single action: request a free quote.

The brand system

Elegant and earthy, drawn from the savannah. Premium without shouting.

The identity pairs a refined serif with a warm, natural palette: a deep olive green, a safari gold, and a soft cream, set over real wildlife photography. A hand-built emblem, a giraffe, acacia, and rising sun inside a ring, gives the brand a crest-like sense of heritage and care.

The website

Open on a real sighting, build trust, then ask for one thing.

The homepage leads with a full-bleed golden-hour game drive and the promise "Private Kenya Safaris, Planned Around Real Sightings." From there it earns trust the way a considered traveler needs: why travel with Mzingo, handpicked lodges, a few real safaris to picture the trip, and social proof from Tripadvisor and SafariBookings. Every section points at one action, "Get a Free Quote."

It is fully responsive, so the same calm, premium feel holds whether someone is dreaming on a laptop or booking from a phone.
