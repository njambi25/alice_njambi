Brand identity · Web design

TourMode a bold brand for life on the road

I designed the brand identity and marketing site for TourMode, a tour-management platform for touring musicians and their crews. The goal: a brand that feels as road-ready as its users, and a landing page that makes a busy tour manager stop and request access.

01 · The brief

Earn a tour manager's trust instantly

Nothing lost between the spreadsheet, the group chat, and the printout taped to a dressing-room door.

TourMode pulls tour planning, advancing, day sheets, and settlements into one app. Its audience is tour managers and crews: practical people, moving fast, deciding in seconds whether a tool is worth their time.

The brief was a brand that earns their trust instantly. It had to feel built for the road, confident and no-nonsense, and a landing page that turns a skeptical scroll into a request for access.

02 · Brand system

Loud where it counts, quiet everywhere else

Built to read at a glance in a dark venue. The system is dark by default, the way a stage is. A heavy, condensed display face sets the headlines like a tour poster; an electric blue drives the actions; and a warm amber marks the touring-world details. Light, spacious sections give the product room to breathe between the loud moments.

03 · The website

A cinematic open, then an honest walk-through

The homepage opens like a show: a black hero, the tagline set huge, and a live show-day status bar ticking through load-in, soundcheck, doors, and set. From there it gets practical. Four plain steps, Plan, Advance & Crew, Run the Show, and Settle, lay out what the app does, and a side-by-side "old way versus new way" makes the pitch without a word of hype.

Because the app is invitation-only for now, every call to action points at one thing: request early access. The whole page is built to earn that single click.
